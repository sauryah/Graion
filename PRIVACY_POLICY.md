# Privacy Policy for Lical

**Effective Date:** August 18, 2026

Lical ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our data practices regarding the Lical mobile application ("the App").

### 1. Information Collection and Use
Lical is designed as a 100% offline utility and engineering calculator.

- **No Personal Information Collected:** We do not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal identifiable information (PII) such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, location data, or device identifiers.
- **No Analytics or Tracking:** We do not use third-party tracking, telemetry, crash-reporting SDKs, or analytics services.
- **No Advertisements:** The App contains no advertising networks or third-party advertising cookies.

### 2. Local Device Storage
All data generated by you while using the App (including calculation history, custom wire drawing die schedules, and user preferences) is stored locally on your device using Android Room and Jetpack DataStore. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third party.

You can permanently delete all stored calculation history and local preferences at any time directly within the App's **Settings** screen.

### 3. Device Permissions
The App requests only standard device capabilities necessary for core features:
- **Vibration/Haptics:** Used solely to provide tactile keypad feedback when typing.
The App does not request Internet permission (`android.permission.INTERNET`) or access to sensitive device sensors.

### 4. Children's Privacy
Because the App does not collect any personal data, it is safe for users of all ages, including children under 13.

### 5. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected with a revised "Effective Date" at the top of this document.
